# Project Scope

## Problem

CS teams handle large volumes of customer conversations, but manually identifying recurring VOC patterns and operational improvement opportunities is slow and inconsistent.

## Objective

Build an AI-assisted CX Operations workflow that converts raw customer inquiries into structured, measurable operational insights.

## v0.1 Scope

- CSV-based VOC ingestion
- Major/minor category classification
- Priority classification
- Sentiment classification
- Human-review flag
- Exportable results

## Out of Scope for v0.1

- Production CRM integration
- Automatic customer replies
- Real customer personal data
